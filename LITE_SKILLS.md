# EmberAdventuresLite authoring skills

The two Lite skills are version 2.0.0 and target the October 22, 2026 engine rebuild.

- `skills/emberadventureslite-character/SKILL.md`: character/animal format, ten body scales, outfits, state and canonical exports.
- `skills/emberadventureslite-story/SKILL.md`: story structure, player options, cast, objectives, rewards and endings. Load the character skill with it.
- `downloads/EmberAdventuresLite-skills.zip`: both complete skill folders for installation or use as reference files.

Lite definitions require `lite: true`. Use these skills for Lite files; normal EmberAdventures retains its separate skills and format. Romance, flirting and dating are supported. The repository-backed validator invokes the actual Lite checkout's canonical validators and reports errors without repairing the input.

Reference character/story exports, field snapshots and both routes of the short example story were checked against the rebuilt engine. Skills are authoring instructions; they do not include or replace the game.
